These terms explain how Mogli CRM, the web dashboard and the Android app are used by travel companies and their staff. This is a plain-language draft: have your legal advisor review it and complete the items marked “to be confirmed” before launch.

01Who these terms cover

Mogli CRM is business software used by travel companies (tour operators, destination management companies and travel agents) to manage enquiries, quotations, bookings and payments. These terms apply to the company that holds an account (“the organization”) and to the staff it lets use Mogli CRM.

02The service

Mogli CRM is provided as a web dashboard and an Android app. It helps an organization capture enquiries, build itineraries and quotations, track bookings and suppliers, and record and follow payments.

03Accounts and organizations

  • An account belongs to an organization. The organization’s administrators decide who gets an account and what role each person has.
  • Keep your sign-in details private.
  • The organization is responsible for what its staff do in its account.

04Your data

The organization decides which of its customers’ details are entered and is responsible for them; Mogli CRM stores and processes that data on the organization’s behalf. Data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

How data is collected, used, shared and deleted is explained in the Privacy Policy, which forms part of these terms.

05Acceptable use

Use Mogli CRM only for your travel business and in line with the law. Do not:

  • try to get into another organization’s data or accounts;
  • interfere with the service or try to bypass its security;
  • enter data you have no right to use.

06Fees and payment

Pricing and payment terms for an organization are set out in what is agreed with it when it signs up. To ask about pricing, get in touch. Billing details, taxes and refund terms: to be confirmed.

07Ending your use

To delete an account or its data, follow the steps in the Privacy Policy; accounts and data are deleted within 30 days of a confirmed request.

Suspension, notice periods and data export on closing: to be confirmed.

08Liability and governing law

Limits of liability, warranties, governing law and how disputes are handled: to be confirmed by Mogli’s legal advisor before these terms are published.

09Contact

Questions about these terms: email developer@anantavya.com or use the contact page.
