The problem

What manual lead handover looks like

When someone has to decide who takes each lead, it slows everything down.

1

Leads wait for a decision

A manager has to read every enquiry and pick an owner. In the meantime, the traveller is waiting for a reply.

2

Uneven workload

Some people get five new leads a day and others get none, because nobody is keeping count.

3

Nobody knows who has what

Without a clear owner on every lead, two people reply or nobody does.

How it works

Assign leads without lifting a finger

1

Step 1

Set your rules

Decide how leads are shared across your team, so each enquiry reaches someone who knows the trip.

2

Step 2

Owners appear on every lead

New leads show the team member and their role right in the list. Leads that cannot be placed are marked Unassigned instead of sitting unnoticed.

3

Step 3

Watch the team’s numbers

See leads, conversions and booked value for the period, and compare how the work is spread across the team.

What you get
