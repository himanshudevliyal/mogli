See Every Payment, Owed and Due, in Real Time

One dashboard shows the money to collect from customers and the money to pay suppliers for every booking, so you always know where cash stands and who to chase.

Request a Demo See how it works

The problem

What payments look like without tracking

Cash problems usually start as small things nobody wrote down.

1

Dues tracked in notes

Instalments and due dates sit in chats and sheets, so reminders depend on memory.

2

Supplier payments forgotten

What you owe suppliers is separate from what customers owe you, so the cash picture is always partial.

3

Overdue found too late

Without a live view, a payment that is overdue is only noticed when someone asks.

How it works

Cash in view, always

1

Step 1

Total at the top

The dashboard leads with money to collect from customers and money to pay suppliers, with what is overdue and what is due in the next seven days.

2

Step 2

Open any booking

Every booking lists incoming payments from the customer and outgoing payments to suppliers, each with its due date and status.

3

Step 3

Settle with suppliers

Move to the supplier ledger to see what each supplier has billed, what is paid and what is outstanding.

What you get

What payment tracking gives you

Know what is paid, pending and overdue without asking.

Collect and pay, side by side

Money to collect from customers and money to pay suppliers sit together, with overdue amounts and the next seven days called out.
