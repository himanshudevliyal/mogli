The problem

Where leads leak when capture is manual

Three things go wrong quietly, and each one costs a booking.

1

Slow response time

Someone has to notice the enquiry, copy it somewhere and pass it on. By the time anyone replies, the traveller has moved on to the next operator.

2

No view of your leads

Which are new, which are being worked and which are stuck? When leads live in inboxes and sheets, nobody can say without asking around.

3

Good leads fall through

A busy day, a missed message, a lead that was never logged. Human error is rarely noticed until the customer books elsewhere.

How it works

One change that makes lead handling calm

1

Step 1

Capture automatically

Enquiries from your website and ads arrive in the lead list as they happen. Enquiry emails are read for you: the ones Mogli is sure about become leads, and the rest wait for your review.

2

Step 2

Centralise and organise

Every lead sits in one list with its contact, trip details, source and team member. Filter by source, see what is unassigned and open anything that needs attention.

3

Step 3

Track every stage

See how many leads came in, how many were quoted and how many converted for any period, so you know which sources and people actually bring in bookings.
