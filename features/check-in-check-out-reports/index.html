The problem

What arrivals look like without it

Hotel movements are easy to miss when they live inside individual bookings.

1

Arrivals found by searching

To know who arrives tomorrow, someone opens booking after booking.

2

Hotels and guests out of step

Without a daily list, a hotel may not know a guest is coming or leaving.

3

Contact details scattered

The guest phone number and stay dates are in different places when you need them most.

How it works

A daily list your team can act on

1

Step 1

Choose the days

Pick any date range and see every check-in and check-out that falls in it.

2

Step 2

Read the list

Each row shows the date, the movement, the hotel, the guest, the booking, the travellers and the guest’s phone number.

3

Step 3

Download or share

Take the list as a CSV for your ground team or the hotel.
