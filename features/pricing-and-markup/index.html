Price Every Trip With Your Margin in Plain Sight

Mogli keeps cost and sale price side by side, so you know what each booking earns before you confirm and after you collect.

Request a Demo See how it works

The problem

What pricing looks like without it

Margin is easy to protect and easy to lose.

1

Cost and price in different files

The supplier cost is in one sheet and the quote in another, so margin is a calculation nobody has time for.

2

Discounts given blind

Without the margin in front of you, a discount that looks small can wipe out the profit.

3

Surprises after the trip

Costs change late, and you only find out when the books are closed.

How it works

Know what each trip earns

1

Step 1

Set cost and price

Each service carries its supplier cost, and the quote carries your sale price, so the margin is known from the start.

2

Step 2

Read the profit report

See sale, cost, profit and margin for every booking in the period, with filters for destination, salesperson and brand.

3

Step 3

Spot trips that can still move

Bookings whose numbers can still change are flagged separately, so final profit is never confused with expected profit.

What you get

What you can see

Margin as a number, not a guess.

Profit by booking

The profit report lists every booking with sale, cost, profit and margin, and totals what is final and what can still move.

Filter the way you think

Slice profit by what matters to you.

  • Period
  • Destination
  • Salesperson
  • Brand

Final vs can still move

Profit that is final is kept apart from profit that may change.

Multi-currency aware

Costs in another currency are converted at today’s rate for the report.

CSV download

Take the booking list into a spreadsheet when you need to.
